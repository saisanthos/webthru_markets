Stock market today: India and the US at a glance

This page brings the markets that Indian and American investors follow most into a single view of the stock market today. At the top, the ticker tracks the Sensex, the S&P 500, the Nasdaq 100 and the Dow Jones, as well as the rupee, gold, crude oil and Bitcoin. Below it, the market overview lets you switch between India, the USA, commodities, crypto and forex.

How to follow the stock market today

First, look at the heatmaps. Each block is a company, sized by its market value and coloured by its latest move, so you can see at a glance which sectors are rising or falling. Next, check the economic calendar for events such as US jobs data, inflation figures and Reserve Bank of India rate decisions, because these often move both markets. Finally, open any stock or index for its full chart, company data and technical ratings from 1 minute to monthly.

About the data

Charts and prices come from TradingView. For example, US index levels are FOREX.com CFD quotes that closely track the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and Dow Jones, while US stock prices are delayed. Indian stock prices, on the other hand, come from BSE and update at the end of each trading day. In addition, currencies, commodities and crypto update in real time. For more detail on each country, see the India page and the USA page.
