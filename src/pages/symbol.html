Stock technical analysis: how to read this page

Technical analysis studies price and volume to describe a stock's trend and momentum. The technical ratings on this page combine two groups of signals. Moving averages compare the price with its average over periods such as 10, 50 and 200 sessions. Oscillators, such as the RSI, MACD and stochastic, measure momentum and whether a move looks stretched.

Each signal votes buy, neutral or sell, and the summary gauge adds up the votes. You can switch the timeframe from 1 minute to 1 month, because a stock can be in a short-term downtrend while its long-term trend is still up. For Indian stocks, use the daily view, since BSE prices update at the end of each trading day.

Important
