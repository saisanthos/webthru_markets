Indian stock market overview

The Sensex tracks 30 of the largest companies listed on BSE, so it is a quick way to judge the mood of the Indian stock market. In addition, the chart at the top shows the Sensex next to the US dollar to rupee rate, since currency moves and foreign investment flows are watched closely alongside the index.

How to follow the Indian stock market

To begin with, use the stock lists to compare leading companies by sector, such as banks and IT services. Then the Sensex heatmap shows every company in the index, sized by its market value and coloured by its latest move. For a wider view, the screener ranks Indian stocks by market capitalisation and lets you filter by valuation, performance and technical rating. Also, the BSE movers list shows the most active stocks, the top gainers and the top losers.

Indian stocks that trade in New York

Several Indian companies, including Infosys, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Wipro and Dr. Reddy's, also trade in New York as American depositary receipts. Because their US prices move during US trading hours, they can hint at sentiment before the Indian market opens the next morning.

About Indian stock market data

Indian prices come from BSE and update once after the market closes. However, NSE prices, including the Nifty 50, are not available on this page yet. Meanwhile, the economic calendar lists Indian events such as Reserve Bank of India repo rate decisions and inflation data. For the US side, see the USA page.
